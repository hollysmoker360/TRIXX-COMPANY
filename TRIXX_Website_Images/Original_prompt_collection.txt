How to use this guide
Use these prompts in Midjourney, ChatGPT Image Generator, DALL·E, Leonardo AI, Adobe Firefly, or another
image-generation tool. Generate images without text or logos, then add website headlines, buttons, interface
labels, and the TRIXX logo in the website itself.

Obsidian black (#151B1A): premium architectural backgrounds. Warm ivory (#F4F0E8): clean editorial sections.
Sage green (#A9C5AD): technology and sustainability accents. Muted bronze (#B99A75): luxury detailing.

Premium architectural editorial photography, photorealistic materials, sophisticated African contemporary design
influence, natural cinematic lighting, realistic shadows, refined composition, subtle sage-green accents, warm
neutral color grading, luxury technology brand art direction, realistic proportions, high dynamic range, no
oversaturated colors, no cheap CGI appearance, no random text, no watermarks, no logos, no distorted
architecture, professional commercial photography.
Append the reusable style instruction to each image prompt when the generator benefits from a consistent style. Some generators
may follow it better if it is placed at the end.

17. Practical implementation recommendations
• Use WebP or AVIF for website photographs where supported, and compress images before publishing.
• Keep color grading consistent across the site; avoid mixing cold blue imagery with warm ivory and sage-green
imagery.
• Use subtle motion: small parallax on the homepage hero, gentle image zoom on collection cards, and smooth
crossfades between architectural images.
• Use real 3D models or frontend animations for interactive house customization. A generated video or still image
cannot provide true interactive controls.
• Keep text outside images. Render headings, pricing, property details, and navigation as HTML.
• Do not present AI-generated images as proof of completed projects or as real customers. Use authentic
photographs for actual projects, customers, team members, and case studies.
