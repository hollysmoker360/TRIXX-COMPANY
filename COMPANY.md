# TRIXX — Company Profile

## Overview

TRIXX Smart Living Limited is a Kenya-based property and technology company founded in Nairobi in 2016. TRIXX brings together homebuilding, property services, and connected technology under the promise: **“Homes that think, farms that thrive.”**

The company’s work is centered on making homes and farms easier to manage, helping people find and manage property, and designing buildings that can adapt as technology changes.

## What TRIXX does

- **Builds smart homes:** Offers architectural home collections designed with connected systems and provision for future upgrades.
- **Supports property transactions:** Provides property listings and services for buyers, renters, owners, and landlords.
- **Connects buildings and farms:** Supplies technology for home monitoring, energy, water, security, and irrigation.
- **Provides digital tools:** Connects its services through a suite of apps for residents, property users, and schools.

## Products

- **TRIXX Home** — Tools for monitoring and managing connected home systems.
- **TRIXX Farm** — Tools for farm irrigation and related operations.
- **TRIXX Rent** — Rental and tenancy management.
- **TRIXX Market** — Property discovery and listings.
- **TRIXX School** — Digital services for schools and their communities.

## How TRIXX works

TRIXX’s approach is guided by three principles:

1. **Build for change.** Design buildings and systems so they can be upgraded without unnecessary disruption.
2. **Measure before making claims.** Communicate performance using evidence from installed systems.
3. **Be clear about the service.** Help customers understand what TRIXX builds, what it lists, and what technology is included.
